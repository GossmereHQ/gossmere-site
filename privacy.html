This website

This Gossmere website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts or account registration. The Services pages offer an optional client-side enquiry form which prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not itself transmit, receive, or store the form contents. Information is sent only if you decide to send that email through your email provider.

Email contact

If you contact Gossmere by email (including email prepared through the Services form), the information you choose to send is used to respond to your enquiry, assess requested services and provide related support where applicable. Avoid providing sensitive personal information. Email providers may process messages under their own applicable terms and privacy notices. You can contact us at the address below for enquiries about your data.

Hosting

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure the website in accordance with its own privacy practices.

Product privacy

Privacy information specific to individual Gossmere products will be provided with the relevant product and sales materials where applicable.
