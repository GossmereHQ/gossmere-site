SCOPE / FIT

From local businesses to larger organisations.

For a small company, a usable website and better enquiries may be the priority. Larger organisations may need stronger integration, data governance and process continuity. The approach remains grounded in the actual problem.

PROCESS / QUALITY

Clarity from initial assessment to delivery.

Gossmere approaches each project through its objectives, existing processes and operational requirements. Architecture, accessibility, security and maintainability guide technical decisions. Scope, responsibilities and validation criteria are defined before development, keeping solutions useful and sustainable over time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. The scope is based on actual needs and resources, without unnecessary complexity.

Can you work internationally?

We welcome international enquiries and assess working language, requirements and feasibility before committing.

How is a project priced?

Pricing depends on scope, content, integrations and ongoing support. We prepare a defined proposal after an initial discussion.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or AI mentions?

No. We can improve technical and editorial readiness, but indexing, rankings and AI citations are controlled by external platforms.
