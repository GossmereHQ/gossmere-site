WHAT WE REVIEW

Evidence-led SEO, not a list of keywords.

01 / DISCOVERABILITY

Crawling and indexing

Robots directives, sitemaps, canonical URLs and indexing signals that affect how search engines interpret your pages.

02 / SITE STRUCTURE

Architecture and internal links

Information architecture, page hierarchy and internal links between informative content and core commercial services.

03 / SEARCH INTENT

Content and relevance

How clearly your pages address actual user needs, describe your offer and avoid overlap or unnecessary duplication.

04 / EXPERIENCE

Usability and contact flow

Mobile experience, basic accessibility, observed performance and the path from a relevant page to a meaningful enquiry.

WHO IT IS FOR

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before requesting an SEO audit.

Can you work with businesses outside Italy?

We welcome international enquiries. Feasibility, working language, project scope and collaboration arrangements are confirmed before engagement.

Can an audit guarantee rankings, traffic or AI citations?

No. An audit identifies issues and opportunities. Search rankings, traffic and mentions in AI-assisted answers depend on systems and factors outside our control.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. We assess whether meaningful improvements can be made to the existing site before recommending major changes.
